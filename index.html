Tesla Nav

A personal iPhone app that sends ride destinations to the owner's Tesla.

What it does

When the owner taps Navigate in the Uber Driver app, Tesla Nav receives the pickup or drop-off coordinates and sends them to the navigation system of the owner's own Tesla using the Tesla Fleet API.

Who uses it

One person: the vehicle owner. The app is not distributed on the App Store and has no other users.

Privacy

  • No server stores data. This site only hosts the app's public key and the sign-in return page.
  • Tesla sign-in tokens are kept in the iPhone's Keychain and are sent only to Tesla.
  • Destinations are sent only to Tesla and kept in a short history on the phone.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party sharing.
